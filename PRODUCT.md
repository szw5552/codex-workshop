# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Teams that need to turn one talk, workshop, or lecture recording into a readable shared record. Readers arrive after the event and want the gist first, then a reliable edited transcript they can search, cite, and revisit.

## Product Purpose

This project converts one `upload/` folder of talk audio and photos into a browsable archive. Success means a team member can drop in files, run one command, and get a polished event page with images, a concise briefing, an explanatory infographic, and the full cleaned transcript.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, calm, archival. The page should feel like a considered field report: image-led, readable for long sessions, and confident without looking like a dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative glass cards, purple-on-white AI defaults, dense admin tooling, and fake magazine affectations that make the transcript harder to read.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the event, not the tool: photos and the talk title establish context before mechanics.
- Make the summary scannable: readers should understand the talk before committing to the full transcript.
- Treat long text as a first-class interface: line length, rhythm, contrast, and anchors matter.
- Keep automation visible but quiet: processing artifacts should be traceable without cluttering the page.
- Preserve originals: generated pages are convenient, archived source files remain the record.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize high readability over decorative density. Use semantic HTML, strong contrast, comfortable Traditional Chinese line-height, meaningful image alt text, reduced-motion alternatives, and keyboard-friendly links.
